Our Story

Homely Recipes, Ready to Deliver

RBS Sweets & Snacks runs on the same shelves you see here — packed tight with packets, restocked every day. What started as a neighbourhood snack counter now supplies homes, shops and bulk orders across Coimbatore.

Every packet is prepared with the same care, whether it's a small home order or a wholesale delivery going out on our van.
